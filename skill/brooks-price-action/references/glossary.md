# Brooks Price Action — Glossary

Compact definitions in my own words. **Count** is the number of case-insensitive regex matches across all 5,423 corpus posts (text and headings, all years and categories). Short tokens (ii, ioi, oo, BLSH) were matched case-sensitively. Counts are approximate and show how much a term is used, not its importance. Very generic words (breakout, pullback) are included because the reports lean on them. Related terms are grouped together.

## Market state & cycle

| Term | Count | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Trading range (TR) | 36,907 | Sideways, two-sided area of "confusion" where both sides see value. About 80% of breakout attempts from a TR fail. |
| Breakout (BO) | 31,305 | A move beyond a prior high, low, or pattern edge. Starts a new trend if it gets follow-through. |
| Pullback (PB) | 17,150 | A temporary counter-trend move. After ~20 bars it is treated as a TR (neutral). |
| Channel | 15,042 | The trend phase after the spike: sloped, with pullbacks. Tight or broad. |
| Follow-through | 11,230 | Bars after a breakout or signal that continue in its direction. The key confirmation. |
| Always In (AI) | 10,416 | The side you would hold if you had to be in the market at all times. Flips on a strong, believed breakout. |
| Always In Long / Short (AIL / AIS) | 1,297 / 856 | Current Always In direction. Its strength is graded separately. |
| Market cycle | — | Breakout (spike) → channel → trading range → next breakout. |
| Breakout Mode (BOM) | 2,375 | Balanced pattern with both a buy and a sell setup. About 50/50 direction, and the first breakout fails ~50% of the time. |
| Tight trading range (TTR) | 3,889 | Small, overlapping bars with dojis. A BOM pattern, poor for stop entries, and most traders should wait. |
| Trading range day | 2,298 | A day dominated by two-sided action. Fade breakouts and scalp with limit orders. |
| Trend day | 3,556 | A day that trends from early on, with few pullbacks. |
| Trend from the open | 814 | A trend beginning on the first bars that lasts much of the day (about 20–25% of days). |
| Small pullback trend (SPT) | 1,038 | A strong trend whose pullbacks are brief (1–3 bars) and shallow. Counter-trend trades get trapped. |
| Trending trading range | 290 | A day of stacked TRs, each shifted in the trend direction. |
| Neutral | 659 | Bull and bear odds roughly equal (e.g., a TR older than ~20 bars). |
| Limit order market | 216 | A market where fading with limit orders (buy low, sell high) beats stop entries. Typical of TRs. |
| Bull/bear leg in a TR | 516 | A strong-looking move that is probably just one swing within a range. |
| Sideways to up / down | 5,818 | Report phrase for an expected drift or TR with a directional bias. |

## Bar & candle descriptors

| Term | Count | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Bull bar / bear bar | 16,568 | Close above or below the open. A "body" shows control. |
| Trend bar | 2,029 | A bar with a meaningful body in one direction. The opposite of a doji. |
| Bull/bear body | 2,052 | The body's color matters for signal-bar quality. A bear body on a buy signal bar is weak. |
| Doji | 3,439 | Little or no body. Means balance, and makes a weak signal bar. |
| Tail | 4,452 | A wick. A tail at the trade-side end of a signal bar shows opposing pressure. |
| Signal bar | 5,799 | The bar before entry. The entry is a stop 1 tick beyond it. |
| Entry bar | 2,508 | The bar on which the order fills. |
| Follow-through bar | 1,070 | The bar after the entry or breakout bar. A bad one (doji or opposite body) warns of a trap. |
| Reversal bar | 1,642 | A bar that opens one way and closes strongly the other way. A reversal signal in context. |
| Inside bar (i) | 2,241 | High ≤ the prior high and low ≥ the prior low. Contraction. |
| Outside bar (o) | 2,281 | Exceeds both the prior high and the prior low. Meaningful only with context. |
| ii | 328 | Two consecutive inside bars. A BOM setup. |
| iii | 3 | Three consecutive inside bars. A rare, extreme contraction. |
| ioi | 529 | Inside–outside–inside. A BOM setup, often a flag. |
| oo | 106 | Two consecutive outside bars. A BOM setup. |
| Surprise bar | 518 | An unexpectedly big trend bar. It usually gets at least a small 2nd leg its way. A "Major Surprise Bar" makes the opposite trend unlikely. |
| Consecutive bull/bear bars | 3,635 | A streak of trend bars. Shows strength early and climax late. |
| Gap bar (MA gap bar, MAG) | 324 (MAG 106) | A bar entirely above or below the EMA. Shows trend strength. |
| 20 Gap Bar (20GB) | 123 | 20+ consecutive bars not touching the EMA, so the first pullback to the EMA is a setup (test of the extreme ~75%). |
| Buy/Sell the close (BTC/STC) | 239 / 107 | Entering at the close of a strong trend bar. Typical in spikes. |
| Climactic | 1,853 | Unusually big, or one of too many trend bars. Prone to exhaustion. |

## Entries, counts, setups

| Term | Count | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| High 1 (H1) | 1,220 | First attempt to resume a bull trend after a pullback: a bar trades above the prior bar's high. Good only in strong trends. |
| High 2 (H2) | 709 | Second attempt up. The classic bull-flag or TR-bottom buy, often near the EMA. |
| High 3 / High 4 | 135 | Third or fourth attempt. Usually a wedge bull flag (H3) or a later variant. |
| Low 1 (L1) | 683 | First attempt to resume a bear trend. Weak at the bottom of a TR. |
| Low 2 (L2) | 548 | Second attempt down. The classic bear-flag or TR-top sell. |
| Low 3 / Low 4 (Low 5) | 218 | Third or later attempt. Often a wedge top. "Low 5" when one leg is itself a wedge. |
| Reset the count | ~10 | A leg strong enough that traders start counting attempts or pushes again from it. |
| Second entry | 590 | A second signal in the same direction after the first one failed. Higher probability. |
| Stop entry / stop order | 3,558 | Buying above or selling below a bar on a stop. Recommended for beginners. |
| Limit order (entry) | 2,710 | Fading: buying below or selling above bars. Used in TRs, by experienced traders. |
| Scale in | 1,158 | Adding at better prices with a wide stop. Raises probability and also risk. |
| Wide stop | 313 | A stop beyond the swing extreme, sized small. The alternative to tight, low-probability stops. |
| Skunk stop | 19 | An "in-between" stop near a 50% pullback. Usually the worst choice. |
| Swing (trade) | 11,100 | Target ≥ 2× risk, at least two legs, holds through one pullback. ~40% win rate is fine. |
| Scalp | 4,262 | Target ≈ risk, quick exit, no pullback allowed. Needs ≥60% probability. |
| Trader's equation (TE) | 148 | Probability × reward vs (1 − probability) × risk. Must be positive. |
| 40–60 rule | 3 | Most equal risk/reward trades have a 40–60% probability. Strong breakouts are the exception. |
| 80% rule | 44 | About 80% of TR breakout attempts fail, and about 80% of trend reversal attempts fail. |
| Actual risk | 33 | The real drawdown from entry, as opposed to the initial stop distance. |
| BLSH / BLSHS | 844 | Buy Low, Sell High (and Scalp). The TR playbook. |
| Buy/Sell setup, signal | 1,233 / 7,827 | A pattern that has a signal bar ready, or has triggered. |
| Trap / trapped | 2,063 | Traders caught on the wrong side, whose exits fuel the move against them. |
| Bull trap / bear trap | 156 / 222 | A failed breakout that traps breakout traders. |
| Second-leg trap | 244 | In a TR, a strong-looking 2nd leg that fails to break major S/R. Usually a climax. |
| Fade | 189 | Trading against a move, expecting it to fail. |

## Trend & reversal patterns

| Term | Count | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Major trend reversal (MTR) | 3,926 | A trend reversal after a strong counter-move and a test: HL/LL at bottoms, LH/HH at tops. A double-top/bottom type pattern. About 40% become swings or trends. |
| Higher-low / lower-low MTR | 999 / 231 | Bull MTR variants. The test of the low is above or below the prior low. |
| Lower-high / higher-high MTR | 1,032 / 406 | Bear MTR variants. |
| Minor reversal | 675 | A reversal likely to become only a pullback or TR leg, not a new trend. The usual outcome of a first reversal. |
| Trend reversal / resumption | 4,745 / 856 | The two outcomes traders weigh at every pullback or TR. |
| Wedge | 11,054 | Three pushes in a channel, usually converging. A reversal or flag pattern. Expect ~10 bars, 2 legs, a ~50% retracement. |
| Wedge top / bottom | 2,705 / 1,597 | A three-push reversal pattern at a trend extreme. |
| Wedge bull / bear flag | 937 / 520 | A three-push pullback (H3/L3) that resumes the trend. |
| Three pushes | 329 | Same idea as a wedge, used when the channel isn't converging. |
| Micro wedge | 300 | A wedge made of single bars, often hidden inside a "V". |
| Parabolic wedge | 1,712 | An accelerating, climactic three-push move. Often ends in a TR or endless pullback. |
| Final flag | 803 | A late-trend flag whose breakout fails and reverses the trend. |
| Expanding triangle | 897 | Higher highs and lower lows (broadening). A reversal at tops and bottoms. |
| Triangle | 3,466 | Converging TR. A Breakout Mode pattern. |
| Double top / bottom (DT/DB) | 5,989 / 4,026 | A second test of an extreme. The core of MTRs and TR edges. |
| Micro double top / bottom (MDT/MDB) | 1,085 / 715 | Two nearby bars testing the same high or low. Often the minimum needed to reverse a tight channel. |
| Double-bottom bull flag / double-top bear flag | 718 / 1,003 | A pullback with two tests that resumes the trend. |
| Head and shoulders | 1,073 | Viewed as a TR or MTR variant. About 60% of H&S-top breakdowns fail. |
| Spike and channel | 620 | Strong spike, then a weaker channel. The channel's start is usually retested (~75% opposite breakout). |
| Spike | 1,395 | The breakout phase: consecutive big trend bars. |
| Endless pullback | 210 | A slightly sloping tight TR grinding against the trend. A way trends end without an MTR. |
| Island top / bottom | 880 | A gap-separated cluster at an extreme. Usually a minor reversal. |
| Climax (buy/sell) | 6,327 (3,886 / 2,125) | An over-extended move. Expect ≥2h sideways-to-opposite next (~75%). |
| Exhaustion / exhaustive | 970 | A climax that ends a move. A late big bar is more likely exhaustion than a measuring gap. |
| Vacuum (buy/sell vacuum) | 990 | Fast, low-resistance move toward a magnet (e.g., a TR edge), often followed by a reversal there. |
| V-bottom/top | — | Al says it is "almost always something else" (micro DB, wedge, final flag) on another timeframe. |
| TBTL (Ten Bars, Two Legs) | 502 | Minimum expected correction after a good reversal setup. |
| Big Up, Big Down / Big Down, Big Up | 260 | A big move then a big opposite move. Creates confusion and TR odds. |
| Opening reversal | 434 | An early move that reverses from S/R, often making the day's high or low. |
| Cup and handle / ledge | 129 / 149 | Minor TR shapes. A ledge is a flat set of equal highs or lows acting as a magnet. |

## Support, resistance, targets

| Term | Count | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| EMA / moving average | 12,624 | 20-bar EMA (and 60-min EMA on the 5-min chart). Dynamic S/R and a magnet. |
| Measured move (MM) | 5,856 | Projected target equal to a prior leg, range height, or spike height. |
| Leg 1 = Leg 2 | 136 | MM where the second leg equals the first. |
| Measuring gap | 410 | A breakout gap expected to sit at the middle of the move. Projects an MM. |
| Breakout gap / exhaustion gap | 193 / 172 | A gap that starts a trend vs one that ends it. |

| Breakout point | 1,729 | The level broken. Its test (breakout pullback/test) is a common entry. |
| Breakout pullback / test | 145 / 167 | A return to the breakout point that holds. |
| Magnet | 2,667 | Any price likely to be tested: prior extremes, MM, EMA, opens/closes, round numbers, gaps. |
| Big Round Number (BRN) | 1,345 | Psychological level (e.g., 3000, 4000) that acts as a magnet. |
| Support / resistance | 5,060 / 3,865 | Prices where buyers or sellers are expected. Al's main tool. |
| Trend line / trend channel line | 3,044 / 489 | Line under the lows or over the highs, and its parallel. An overshoot of the channel line warns of a climax. |
| Trend channel line overshoot | 273 | A move beyond the channel line that often reverses. |
| Neckline | 517 | Breakout line of a DT/DB or H&S. |
| 50% pullback | — | Very common retracement target where strong traders enter. |
| Major lower high / higher low | 977 | The swing that must be broken to confirm a trend change. |
| Buy zone / sell zone | 392 | An area where the odds favor fading in one direction. |
| Higher high / higher low / lower high / lower low | 803 / 3,167 / 4,669 / 545 | Swing structure that defines trend and MTR variants. |
